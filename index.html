My academic interests focus on Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Computer Vision, Intelligent Systems, and Software Engineering.

Driven by curiosity and a passion for innovation, I enjoy developing technology-driven solutions that address real-world challenges. I am particularly interested in building intelligent systems that combine software, data, and automation to create practical and impactful applications.

Over the years, I have worked on projects spanning web development, database management, machine learning, and intelligent automation. I continuously explore emerging fields such as Deep Learning, Edge AI, Robotics, Cloud Computing, and Intelligent Automation to expand my technical expertise and research capabilities.

As an aspiring researcher and developer, I am committed to continuous learning, innovation, and problem-solving. My goal is to contribute to the advancement of Artificial Intelligence and intelligent computing systems while developing technologies that create meaningful and lasting impact in society.
